Red, Pink or Sore Eyes?

Quick, expert relief for irritated eyes

Home › Eye Care Services › Red, Pink or Sore Eyes

Think of your Optometrist first for:

  • Sore, red, or itchy eyes
  • Treatment of "pink eye" and other bacterial infections
  • Removal of foreign bodies from the eye
  • Treatment of eye allergies or burns
  • Emergency eye care

Common Causes

  • Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye) — bacterial, viral, or allergic
  • Eye Allergies — reaction to pollen, dust, pet dander
  • Dry Eye Syndrome
  • Blepharitis — eyelid inflammation
  • Subconjunctival hemorrhage — red patch, usually harmless
