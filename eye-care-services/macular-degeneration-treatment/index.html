Macular Degeneration Treatment

Slowing the progression with early intervention

Home › Eye Care Services › Macular Degeneration Treatment

There is currently no cure for macular degeneration, but treatment options exist that can slow its progress or improve vision.

Dry AMD Treatment

Routine eye exams for early detection. High-dose zinc and antioxidant supplements (AREDS formula) may slow progression if detected early.

Wet AMD Treatment

  • Anti-VEGF injections — most effective current treatment
  • Photodynamic therapy (PDT)
  • Laser surgery
Regular eye exams are the most important step. Our doctors screen for macular degeneration at every comprehensive exam using digital retinal imaging and OCT technology.
