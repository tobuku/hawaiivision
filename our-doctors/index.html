Our Doctors

Experienced, Hawaii-trained optometrists dedicated to your eye health

Home › Our Doctors

Our optometrists take the time to explain the results of your eye exam, describe your options and listen to your eye care needs. Because maintaining good eye health is an ongoing partnership, we encourage you to ask questions and share your health information, which could be vital to monitoring, diagnosing and treating potential vision problems.

👔

Dr. Robb T. Shibayama, O.D.

Doctor of Optometry — Founder

Dr. Robb T. Shibayama was born and raised in Mililani, Hawaii. He graduated from Mililani High School and later attended University of Hawaii at Manoa where he earned a Bachelor’s degree in Psychology, focusing on health psychology. He attended Pacific University College of Optometry in Forest Grove, Oregon where he graduated with distinction earning a Doctor of Optometry degree.

Dr. Robb practices comprehensive medical-based optometry. He is known for his comprehensive approach to managing and maintaining vision and eye health. He routinely performs diabetic and glaucoma testing, medically treats red and/or pink eyes, and is known for his management of dry eye syndrome. Hawaii Vision Associates is one of the largest optometry practices in the state of Hawaii that routinely diagnoses and treats dry eye syndrome.

Throughout his years of practice, Dr. Robb has established himself as a much loved practitioner. He prides himself on his bedside manner and developing close relationships with his patients. In his free time he enjoys singing and playing his ukulele and spending time with his family.

👔

Dr. Wendi N. Harada Shibayama, O.D.

Doctor of Optometry

Dr. Wendi N. Harada Shibayama was born and raised in Honolulu, Hawaii. She graduated from Kaimuki High School and later attended the University of Hawaii at Manoa where she pursued and earned a Bachelor’s degree in Japanese, honoring Japanese culture and language. She attended Pacific University College of Optometry in Forest Grove, Oregon where she graduated with distinction earning a Doctor of Optometry degree.

Dr. Harada Shibayama is a well-respected practitioner known for her meticulous approach to eye and vision care. She believes that the visual system is not an isolated structure but an integral part of our overall body health. She is a caring and empathetic doctor who is always willing to listen, counsel, and answer any questions patients may have regarding eye and vision care.

👔

Dr. Paula H. Imada, O.D., MEd

Doctor of Optometry — Masters of Education

Dr. Paula H. Imada was born and raised in Pearl City, Hawaii. She attended University of Hawaii at Manoa where she earned a Bachelors in Biology and a certificate in Women Studies. She then attended Pacific University where she earned a Doctor of Optometry and a Masters of Education degree.

Dr. Paula Imada practices comprehensive medical-based optometry and specializes in pediatric vision care and vision therapy. Hawaii Vision Associates is one of only a handful of practices offering vision therapy and vision rehabilitation services on the island of Oahu. She also participates in community outreach seminars to educate parents and students about the importance and impact vision has upon education and learning.

In Dr. Imada’s free time, she enjoys spending time with her family and playing with her dogs Butters and Penny.

👔

Dr. Leila M. Miyamoto, O.D.

Doctor of Optometry

Dr. Leila M. Miyamoto was born and raised in Honolulu, Hawaii. She attended University of Hawaii at Manoa where she earned a Zoology degree. She later attended Pacific University College of Optometry in Forest Grove, Oregon where she graduated with a Doctor of Optometry degree in 1983.

As a pioneer for female doctors in Hawaii, Dr. Miyamoto owned and operated two practices before joining the Hawaii Vision Associates family. With over 20 years of experience in the field, Dr. Miyamoto brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to her diagnosis, treatment, and management of her patients. She specializes in multi-focal contact lens fittings and evaluations.

In her free time, Dr. Miyamoto enjoys playing with her bird, Juni-bird, and her turtle, Kubla Khan.

👔

Dr. Heather M. Wawrzenski, O.D.

Doctor of Optometry

Dr. Heather M. Wawrzenski was born and raised in Hayfork, California. She attended University of California at Berkeley where she earned a Bachelor’s in Exercise Physiology. She later attended and graduated from UC Berkeley School of Optometry where she earned a Doctor of Optometry degree. Before graduating, Dr. Wawrzenski worked as an intern at Tripler Army Medical Center in Honolulu, where she fell in love with Hawaii and its people.

With her background in Exercise Physiology, Dr. Wawrzenski brings a unique standpoint to vision and eye care. She is a firm believer in the importance of physical activity and cardiovascular exercise to minimize risk of eye diseases such as glaucoma. In her free time, she enjoys hiking, surfing, and spending time with her family.

Dr. Colby H. Yamamoto, O.D.

Doctor of Optometry

Dr. Colby H. Yamamoto was born and raised in Honolulu, Hawaii and is a graduate of Punahou School. He attended Pacific University where he earned a Bachelors of Science degree in Biology and later a Doctorate of Optometry degree. In his younger years, Dr. Yamamoto worked as an optometric technician, developing a deep appreciation for doctor-patient relationships.

After graduating from Pacific University, Dr. Yamamoto gained several years of experience in a surgical co-management setting managing a wide array of ocular disease. He brings a wealth of knowledge to help treat patients of all ages and backgrounds. In his free time, Dr. Yamamoto enjoys an active lifestyle: hiking, paddle boarding, running, and playing with his dogs.

Dr. Remi A.G. Tokunaga, O.D.

Doctor of Optometry

Dr. Remi A.G. Tokunaga was born and raised on the island of Oahu. She attended Sacred Hearts Academy School and graduated from University of Hawaii at Manoa with a Bachelors of Arts Degree in Biology. While earning her Bachelor’s degree, Dr. Tokunaga interned at Hawaii Vision Associates with Dr. Robb Shibayama.

She then attended and graduated from Midwestern University, Chicago College of Optometry, earning a Doctor of Optometry degree. After graduation, Hawaii Vision Associates welcomed their once intern back with open arms. Dr. Tokunaga is one of eight children and has a close relationship to each sibling. Interestingly, her father, mother, and two siblings are dentists — she is the only optometrist in the family!
