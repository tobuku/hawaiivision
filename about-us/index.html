About Hawaii Vision Associates

Established in 2000, our eye care clinics specialize in caring for all your vision needs. We handle everything from comprehensive eye exams for glasses and/or contact lenses to medical eye exams for diabetes, glaucoma, cataract and eye infections like “pink eye.” By staying current with the latest developments in eye care, prescription lenses and advances in contact lens technology, we can provide you with the most thorough care today and in the years to come.

Hawaii Vision Associates takes pride in building personal, lasting relationships with our patients. We take the time to get to know you, your vision requirements, and your lifestyle needs.

Our Mission

We believe that providing quality eye care to our patients is a two-way street. Our optometrists take the time to explain the results of your eye exam, describe your options and listen to your eye care needs. Because maintaining good eye health is an ongoing partnership, we encourage you to ask questions and share your health information, which could be vital to monitoring, diagnosing and treating potential vision problems.

Conveniently Located

With four locations across Oahu — in Honolulu, Pearl City, Kapolei, and the Kuakini Physician’s Tower — quality eye care has never been more accessible. Our Keeaumoku, Pearl City, and Kapolei offices are located within Sam’s Club and Walmart, but no membership is required to see our doctors.

Our Commitment to Excellence

Eye care professionals specialize in understanding how the human eye really works, as well as diseases and conditions that affect your vision — many of which do not always have obvious symptoms. Our doctors know how age and risk factors affect vision over time, and they understand the benefits and features of corrective lenses, both as eyeglass lenses and contact lenses.

Established in 2000 — Hawaii Vision Associates is one of the largest optometry practices in the state of Hawaii, and one of only a handful of practices offering vision therapy and vision rehabilitation services on the island of Oahu.

Associations & Awards

All of our doctors are proud members of the Hawaii Optometric Association and the American Optometric Association.
