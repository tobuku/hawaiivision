Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your most common questions

Home › FAQ’s
Q: Are you employed by Walmart, Sam’s Club, or Sears Optical?
No. We are independent doctors of optometry. We make no income from selling glasses or contacts, only from providing professional services such as comprehensive exams and treating ocular disease.
Q: How can your fees be lower? Will I receive a quality exam?
We provide a thorough exam with the most advanced diagnostic instruments. Our doctors received the same four-year training as all optometrists and are licensed by the state. Lower overhead means we can pass savings to patients.
Q: How much does an eye exam cost?
Comprehensive eye exam without insurance starts at $108 (tax included). Contact lens exam starts at $192 (tax included).
Q: Will I receive a written prescription after the exam?
Yes. We supply a written prescription and summary of your exam as you leave. The prescription is valid anywhere in the U.S.
Q: Do I need a Sam’s Club or Walmart membership?
No. Our offices are located within Sam’s Club and Walmart stores, but no membership is required to visit our doctors.
Q: Why do I need an annual exam if my vision seems fine?
Vision can change over a 12-month period. A regular eye exam also detects serious conditions like glaucoma, diabetes, and high blood pressure early, before other symptoms appear.
Q: What insurance plans do you accept?
Most major medical and vision plans including HMSA, UHA, Medicare, Tricare, Alohacare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Davis Vision, NVA, MES Vision, VSP, and many union plans. See our Insurance page.
Q: How do I schedule an appointment?
Call your nearest location: Honolulu 808.945.3937, Pearl City 808.456.3937, Kapolei 808.674.4488 — or use our online contact form.
