Welcome to Hawaii Vision Associates

Established in 2000, our eye care clinics specialize in caring for all your vision needs. We handle everything from comprehensive eye exams for glasses and/or contact lenses to medical eye exams for diabetes, glaucoma, cataract and eye infections like “pink eye.”

Hawaii Vision Associates takes pride in building personal, lasting relationships with our patients. We take the time to get to know you, your vision requirements, and your lifestyle needs.
